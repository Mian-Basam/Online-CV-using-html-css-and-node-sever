About

I'm a software engineer who enjoys turning messy, manual processes into reliable systems. At Educative, I work on the platforms and internal tools behind technical learning: content pipelines, LLM-powered generation and review, and developer-facing web apps.

My work spans the full stack. I build product features in Node.js and Next.js, design AI pipelines that produce courses, blogs and practice questions, and own the delivery side with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and observability.

Before industry, I spent five years teaching Data Structures, OOP, Databases and Web Development at PUCIT, FAST and UMT. Teaching still shapes how I engineer: clear interfaces, honest documentation, and code the next person can understand.
